Why pawtner with us

HomeFur All exists to connect local shelters with people ready to help — here's what your shelter gets.

More visibility

Your animals appear in our searchable listings, seen by adopters across the region.

Free listing

No cost to join or maintain your shelter and animal profiles.

Volunteer & donor access

Tap into our pool of volunteers and donors looking for shelters to support.

Community trust

A HomeFur All badge signals credibility to adopters and pawtners alike.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free to list our shelter?

Yes — HomeFur All never charges shelters to join or maintain a listing.

Is it really free to list our shelter?

You'll need to be a registered or community-recognized shelter/rescue with a physical location and basic animal care records.

How long does verification take?

Most applications are reviewed within a week; we may reach out for a short call or site visit.

Can we update our listing later?

Yes, partner shelters can request updates to their profile and animal listings anytime.
